RakibTech Solutions — Electronics Store Sales & Financial Analysis
> A professional Excel-based business intelligence portfolio project for analyzing sales, expenses, profitability, customer activity, product performance, and regional performance.
---
Project Overview
RakibTech Solutions is a fictional electronics retail and e-commerce business used to demonstrate practical Excel, data analysis, and business intelligence skills.
This project analyzes business transactions for January–December 2025 and transforms raw sales and expense data into a structured analytical workbook with dashboards, PivotTables, PivotCharts, data-cleaning checks, interactive analysis, and a documented Power Query workflow.
The project is designed as a portfolio demonstration of how Excel can be used to support business reporting, financial analysis, data preparation, and decision-making.

Business Analysis Areas
1. Sales Performance
Monthly sales are analyzed to understand changes in business activity throughout the year.
2. Category Performance
Sales are compared across the six electronics categories to understand product-category contribution.
3. Regional Performance
The project compares business activity across Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto, Nagoya, and Fukuoka.
4. Customer Analysis
Customer information and customer-type activity are included to support customer-level business analysis.
5. Sales Channel Analysis
The dataset supports comparison between Online and Store sales channels.
6. Payment Analysis
The project includes multiple payment methods such as Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPay, and Bank Transfer.
7. Expense & Profitability Analysis
Operating expenses are analyzed together with sales and cost to calculate gross profit and net profit.

How to Use
Download the Excel workbook.
Open it using Microsoft Excel desktop.
Start with `01_Dashboard`.
Review the KPI summary.
Explore `09_Analysis`.
Review `10_PivotTables` and `11_PivotCharts`.
Use `13_Interactive_Analysis` for filtered analysis.
Review `02_Sales_Data` and `03_Expenses` for the underlying data.
For the best experience, use Microsoft Excel desktop because some Excel features may behave differently in browser-based spreadsheet viewers.
